How it works

Every detection follows the same path, from first signal to alert.

DetectSensors notice something in the monitored area.
IdentifyAI and camera decide what it is.
TrackPosition, direction and distance are followed.
AssessThe system rates how risky it is.
AlertThe security team is told right away.
AERODEF is a detection and monitoring project. It does not jam or disable drones.
